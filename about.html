Our Foundation & Heritage

From Family Sheds to Multi-Million Bird Integrators

AviTech Poultry Systems was established in 2001 by a multidisciplinary team of agricultural mechanical engineers and lifelong poultry specialists. Having managed commercial farms firsthand, our founders were frustrated by the rapid failure rate of conventional poultry equipment: thin electroplated wire cages rusting after three seasons under acidic ammonia vapors, feeding augers warping under high humidity, clogged water nipples causing wet litter and footpad lesions, and catastrophic heat stress mortalities during sudden summer temperature spikes.

Our founding engineering thesis was simple yet uncompromising: commercial poultry equipment must never be treated as disposable tinware—it is precision biological life-support infrastructure.

Over the past quarter-century, we have grown from a specialized metal fabrication facility into a world-renowned manufacturer. Today, our automated feeding pan lines, hot-dip galvanized battery cages, composite tunnel exhaust fans, and intelligent microclimate computers operate across 1,800+ commercial facilities in 42 countries, housing over 45 million birds daily.
