Guaranteed Factory Performance

Manufactured under strict ISO 9001 guidelines with 20-year anti-corrosion hot-dip galvanization and virgin food-grade polymer contact surfaces.

Quick Equipment Highlights:

  • Modular length scalable from 30m to 150m sheds
  • Compatible with all commercial auger silo boot interfaces
  • CE Certified Drive Motors with IP55 dust and washdown sealing

Anti-Waste Feeder Lip

Special curved inner rim prevents birds from flicking feed out into litter, preserving up to 28 kg of feed per flock cycle.

Central Winching

Heavy galvanized cable winch system raises entire lines to the roof in under 2 minutes for tractor litter cleanout.

Overload Protection

Thermal cut-off sensors automatically protect the reduction gear motor in the event of foreign debris or obstruction.

Site Preparation & Civil Requirements

Electrical Requirements

Standard 380V / 3-Phase / 50-60 Hz (220V Single-Phase optional for small sheds). Includes motor thermal protection circuit breaker.

Ceiling Truss Load Capacity

Trusses must support 45 kg per linear meter suspension point. Suspension pulleys spaced every 3 meters along the house length.
