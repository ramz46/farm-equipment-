Operational Phases

The 5 Phases of Our Turnkey Delivery

From initial site inspection to final bird placement certification.

Phase 1: Civil & Utility Pre-Flight Assessment

Our engineers verify floor slab levelness, electrical grounding, water supply volume, and ceiling truss anchor tolerances before equipment containers are unpacked.

Phase 2: Structural Frame & Cage Tier Anchoring

Erection of hot-dip galvanized cage uprights using optical laser levels to ensure 100% horizontal alignment along 100+ meter rows. Installation of PP manure drop belts.

Phase 3: Automated Feed & Water Drive Lines

Threading high-tensile flex auger coils through galvanized feed pipes, wiring end-line optical cutoff sensors, and hanging stainless drinking nipple lines with pressure regulator flush kits.

Phase 4: Ventilation Fans & Cellulose Cooling Pads

Mounting 50-inch butterfly cone exhaust fans, framing Scandinavian wet wall gutters, and sealing all air inlets to create an airtight negative-pressure tunnel environment.

Phase 5: Electrical Commissioning & Farm Staff Training

Connecting the central IoT microclimate computer, calibrating temperature and ammonia probes, simulating high-heat emergency alarms, and training farm staff on daily operation.
