Critical Management Protocols For Maximum Yield

Practical, proven field procedures directly from our agricultural engineers to help you maintain low mortality and optimal flock feed efficiency.

Broiler Management

Broiler FCR Optimization

Adjust pan feeding heights every 48 hours to match bird back heights. Ensure feed pans run empty for 30 minutes daily to stimulate uniform crop cleaning.

  • Day 1–7: Continuous light for chick feeding
  • Day 8+: 4 hours darkness for bone density
Layer Egg Production

Layer Egg Shell Density

Schedule afternoon calcium top-dressing between 2:00 PM and 4:00 PM when eggshell calcification occurs inside the uterus, reducing cracked eggs below 0.3%.

  • Run manure belts daily to remove ammonia
  • Calibrate egg conveyor speed to 18m/min
Chick Brooding (Days 1–14)

Thermal Brooding Curves

Preheat shed concrete to 32°C 24 hours prior to chick placement. Cold litter causes chick gut shut down and uneven flock weights.

  • Target 60–70% relative humidity
  • Check crop fill: 95% full by Hour 24
Water Quality

Biofilm & Nipple Sanitization

Flush water lines weekly with hydrogen peroxide or chlorine dioxide at 2 bar pressure to dissolve slimy biofilm colonies that harbor E. coli.

  • Maintain water pH between 5.8 and 6.8
  • Keep water temp below 22°C in summer
Ventilation Science

Static Pressure Calibration

Calculate air inlet openings so air enters at 4.5–5.5 m/s velocity. In cold weather, this throws incoming air to the ridge apex before descending on birds.

  • Target 20–25 Pa static pressure differential
  • Inspect belt tension on 50" fans monthly
Biosecurity & Hygiene

Strict 3-Zone Perimeter Rule

Enforce a "Danish Entry" anteroom with color-coded boots, dedicated overalls, and hand sanitizers at the doorway of every poultry house.

  • Maintain 14-day downtime between flocks
  • Thermal fog with glutaraldehyde sanitizers

Frequent Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Technical inquiries regarding equipment selection, stocking density, and farm commissioning.

How many birds can be served by one automated pan feeder?

Our 14-grill AviPan broiler feeder is calibrated to comfortably feed 50 to 65 heavy broilers (up to 2.8 kg harvest weight). For lighter birds or pullets, stocking density can be up to 70 birds per pan. We recommend spacing feeder pans at 0.75m to 1.0m intervals along the drive line.

What is the optimal water pressure for poultry nipple lines?

Water pressure should be maintained low: between 10 cm and 15 cm water column for Day 1 chicks to ensure easy trigger activation without splashing. As birds mature, gradually raise pressure to 30 cm – 40 cm water column by Day 35 to provide high volume flow (80–120 ml/min) without leaking.

A-Type vs H-Type layer cages: What is the primary difference?

A-Type cages are arranged in a stepped ladder structure, allowing manure to drop between tiers directly onto a concrete pit floor or basic scraper. They are cost-effective for naturally ventilated or open-sided sheds. H-Type cages are vertically stacked in tiers with automated PP manure belts running directly under every single tier. H-Type allows much higher stocking density in fully enclosed, tunnel-ventilated houses.

How many 50-inch exhaust fans do I need for a 120m x 14m house?

A 120m x 14m x 3m shed contains approximately 5,040 m³ air volume. To achieve a summer air speed of 2.5 m/s across the 42 m² cross-section, you need approximately 105 m³/sec (equivalent to ~222,000 CFM). Since our 50-inch cone fans produce 44,000 CFM at operating static pressure, this house requires 6 tunnel fans for peak summer cooling plus 2 minimum-ventilation sidewall fans.
